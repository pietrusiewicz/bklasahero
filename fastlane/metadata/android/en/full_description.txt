"B-Klasa Hero" (a.k.a. "Sunday League Hero") is a 100% open-source
football game. No ads, no IAP, no telemetry. No internet.

GAMEPLAY:
  * Take 5 penalty kicks against the CPU, then defend 5.
  * Shooting: tap inside the goal frame.
  * Defending: pick direction and height of your dive.
  * Ball physics: gravity, drag, Magnus effect, post and crossbar bounces.
  * Shootout outcome feeds into the league standings.

CAREER:
  * Start at the bottom of Polish football (B klasa) with a
    procedurally-generated club.
  * Each season = 9 rounds × 5 matches (round-robin).
  * Get promoted or relegated — depending on the table.
  * 8 tiers: B klasa → A klasa → Okręgówka → IV liga → III liga →
    II liga → I liga → Ekstraklasa.
  * XP per match and per kick; attributes: Shot Power, Accuracy,
    Control, Curve, Composure, Reach, Reflexes, Reading.

TECHNOLOGY:
  * Engine: C++23 core with a thin JNI bridge.
  * UI: Jetpack Compose + Material 3.
  * 7,800 Polish localities from OpenStreetMap (ODbL).
  * Procedurally-generated club names and match results.
  * Deterministic seed → identical results on every device.

PRIVACY:
The app requests ZERO permissions. It doesn't collect data. It doesn't
send anything over the network. The only thing it stores is your
single-slot save game in a local database.
